      How the numbers work

      Method and assumptions

      Each channel's budget is turned into a forecast with three numbers a media buyer works with every day: what 1,000 impressions cost (CPM), how many people click (CTR) and how many clickers convert (conversion rate).

      impressions = budget ÷ CPM × 1,000
      clicks = impressions × CTR
      conversions = clicks × conversion rate
      CPA = budget ÷ conversions · ROAS = conversions × value ÷ budget

      Spain is modelled with CPMs about 15% higher than Portugal, reflecting a larger, more competitive auction. The objective nudges the assumptions the way bidding strategy does in practice: awareness campaigns buy cheaper reach with lower intent, conversion campaigns pay more per impression for people more likely to act.

      The forecast is linear, so it does not model frequency caps, audience saturation or learning phases. Treat it as a planning tool for a first conversation with a client, then replace the benchmarks with real account data after the first week.

      The copy checker uses each platform's commonly cited character limits and truncation points. Platforms change these often, so confirm against the official ad specs before launch.
